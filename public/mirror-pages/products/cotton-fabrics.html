Cotton Fabrics

Our cotton fabrics are manufactured within a 100–150 GSM range, offering a balanced combination of breathability, durability, and structure. We maintain strict GSM control and finishing standards to ensure uniform texture, clean weave structure, and dependable batch consistency for bulk supply.
